Hire to Retire: Phantom Payroll: Untangling Off-Cycle Payments and Cost-Center Chaos at Meridian Craft Foods

Finance · Hire to Retire · 60 minutes · fictional case

Context

Meridian Craft Foods is a fictional mid-sized specialty food manufacturer with 2,400 employees across three plants and a head office. Over the past 18 months the company grew rapidly through seasonal hiring and two small acquisitions. HR onboarding is handled in a legacy applicant-tracking spreadsheet, while payroll runs in the ERP's HCM/payroll module. Position data, cost-center assignments, and termination records are maintained manually by plant HR coordinators and re-keyed by the central payroll team. Finance has begun monthly labor-cost reviews ahead of a planned refinancing, and the CFO has asked for a rapid diagnostic of why payroll-related costs and corrections keep climbing despite stable headcount.

Challenge

Diagnose why Meridian's payroll costs, off-cycle payment volume, and labor-cost reporting errors are rising despite flat headcount, and recommend a practical, ERP-enabled Hire to Retire improvement that Finance and HR can implement within two quarters.

Symptoms

  • Off-cycle (manual) payroll payments have risen from ~15 to over 110 per month in a year, each requiring manual approval and bank file handling.
  • Monthly labor-cost reports show 6-9% of payroll expense posted to a default 'suspense' cost center, forcing Finance to reallocate manually at close.
  • Internal audit found 14 terminated employees who received at least one paycheck after their separation date in the last two quarters.
  • New-hire first paychecks are late or wrong for roughly 1 in 5 seasonal hires, driving HR hotline volume and some early attrition.
  • Month-end close for payroll-related accounts now takes 6 business days, up from 3, due to reconciliation and reclassification work.

Evidence

  • Payroll Operations Log (last 12 months): Off-cycle payment reasons: 48% 'new hire not in payroll system at cutoff', 27% 'retro pay for late data entry', 15% 'termination processed after pay run', 10% other. Average 11 calendar days between a plant HR action and its entry in the ERP payroll module.
  • GL Account Analysis, Cost Center 9999 (suspense): Suspense postings concentrate in the two acquired plants, whose employees were loaded with placeholder cost centers during migration; 1,180 of 2,400 employee master records have no validated position-to-cost-center mapping in the ERP org structure.
  • Internal Audit Memo IA-24-07: Terminations are initiated on paper forms signed by plant supervisors; median elapsed time from last working day to payroll-effective termination in the ERP is 9 days. Overpayments to 14 separated employees total $41,300, of which $12,800 is deemed unrecoverable.
  • HR Shared-Services Ticket Report: Tickets tagged 'pay incorrect/missing' grew 170% year over year; 62% of these tickets originate from employees hired within the prior 60 days, and resolution requires an average of 3 handoffs between plant HR, central HR, and payroll.
  • ERP Access and Workflow Configuration Review: The ERP's hire, transfer, and termination workflow capabilities are licensed but not activated; all employee master changes are entered centrally by two payroll clerks from emailed spreadsheets, with no automated validation of cost center, position, or effective dates.

Your deliverable

A 5-minute team presentation stating the root-cause diagnosis, a phased ERP-enabled remediation plan for the Hire to Retire process (people, process, and system actions), 3-4 measurable success targets, and the top risks with mitigations.

Agenda

  • 5 min – Facilitator introduces the case, objectives, and deliverable expectations
  • 12 min – Teams read the brief and evidence pack; clarifying questions to facilitator
  • 18 min – Team working session: root-cause diagnosis and ERP-enabled solution design
  • 10 min – Teams finalize success measures, risks, and build the 5-minute presentation
  • 10 min – Team presentations with facilitator challenge questions
  • 5 min – Debrief: facilitator shares model diagnosis and key Hire to Retire lessons
